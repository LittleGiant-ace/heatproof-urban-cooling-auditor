Assumptions & limitations

  • DiD assumes treatment and control would have changed in parallel without the intervention; the pre-trend and placebo checks probe this but cannot prove it.
  • Intervals treat dates as independent; autocorrelation makes them optimistic. Intervals appear only with ≥4 pre and ≥4 post dates.
  • Weather, clouds, season, land-cover change and sensor processing can confound results.
  • Evidence score = data completeness heuristic (equal weights), not statistical confidence or P(intervention worked).
