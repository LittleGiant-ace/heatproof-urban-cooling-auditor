# HeatProof — Submission Write-up
**Problem.** Cities fund trees, parks and cool roofs but rarely show whether local surface temperatures actually changed *relative to* what weather and season would have done anyway. Naive before/after comparisons mislead.
**Solution.** HeatProof audits an intervention with a treatment-vs-control difference-in-differences, then audits the *audit*: it checks pre-intervention trend divergence, runs a placebo date, tests window sensitivity, validates the data, and reports an evidence-completeness score with a visible breakdown.
**Contribution.** Not a heat map or planning dashboard: the distinguishing feature is surfacing *why a cooling estimate may not be trustworthy* (e.g., the bundled misleading dataset shows ≈ −0.9 °C with zero true effect, and HeatProof flags it). Difference-in-differences itself is a standard method; the contribution is packaging it with transparent comparability checks for non-specialists.
**Environmental relevance.** Better evidence on what cools neighbourhoods helps direct limited funds to measures that work. No impact numbers are claimed; all included data are synthetic.
**AWS.** Static client-side app designed for AWS Amplify Hosting (`amplify.yml` included). *Not yet deployed or tested on AWS.* Other AWS services were intentionally omitted as unnecessary for the prototype.
**Honesty/limitations.** Synthetic data only; no satellite integration (path documented in README); no AI model (rule-based analyst); intervals assume independent dates; DiD does not prove causation; land-surface temperature ≠ air temperature or human heat exposure.
